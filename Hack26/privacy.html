Privacy notice

What we collect when you register, why we collect it, and your choices.

1. Who collects your data

Your data is collected by the Hack-O-Week organising committee of your college. For questions, contact the committee using the details on the contact page.

2. What we collect

Through the registration form we collect only what is needed to run the festival:

  • Name, email address and phone number
  • College, department/course and year of study
  • Events you register for, and team details if you enter as a team
  • Your consent to this notice, and the time of submission

We do not collect payment details, government identifiers or photographs through this website.

3. Why we use it

Only to organise Hack-O-Week: to confirm your participation, form teams, plan venues and event capacity, contact you about schedule changes, and produce attendance records for the college.

4. Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a Supabase database hosted in the cloud. Database security rules (Row Level Security) restrict access to authorised administrators of the festival only. The public website cannot read participant details, and other participants cannot see your information.

5. How long we keep it

Registration records are kept for the duration of the festival and for the reporting period agreed with the college, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Retention period to be confirmed

6. Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your registration by contacting the organising committee. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

7. Changes to this notice

Any change will be published on this page with an updated date.

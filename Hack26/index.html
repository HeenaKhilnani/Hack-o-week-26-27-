About the festival

A week built by students, for students.

Hack-O-Week is an annual college festival that treats technology and culture as part of the same creative life. Alongside multi-hour hackathons and technical contests, the festival makes room for performance, art and the joy of organising something together.

Whether you write code, paint sets, choreograph a routine, or prefer solving problems on paper, there is a category designed for you to take part in. Events are open to undergraduates from all departments — you do not need to be a computer science student to join a hackathon team.

The festival is run by a student organising committee with faculty guidance. Exact dates, venues, eligibility rules and any participation fees will be published on this page once they are confirmed.

“Build something that works. Perform something that moves people. Both belong on the same stage.”

  • Two tracks, one festival. Technical events and cultural events run through the same week.
  • For every department. Participation is open to enrolled students across the college.
  • Individual or team. Most events support both — each event card tells you which.
  • Real organising experience. Students also run registration, stages, venues and publicity.

Why take part

Four good reasons to sign up

No promises we cannot keep — just what a festival like this is genuinely good for.

Collaborate

Work with people from other departments and years. Most festival projects start with a team you had not met before.

Learn

Pick up tools, techniques and stagecraft you would not cover in a classroom — by doing, under time pressure.

Create

Bring an idea, a sketch, a routine or a performance to life — the festival needs makers as much as competitors.

Belong

Festivals are how a campus gets to know itself. Volunteer, compete, or simply show up and support your friends.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Anything still undecided is clearly marked so you know to check back later.

How do I register for Hack-O-Week?

Open the registration form, fill in your details, choose the events you want to join and submit. Your entry is saved only when you see the confirmation screen with a reference number — please screenshot it.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes. The form lets you select several events in one submission. If your plans change, contact the organising committee before the deadline.

Which events are running this year? Being finalised

The Events section lists everything currently published by the organisers. Categories include hackathons, technical quizzes, project showcases, gaming, dance, music, drama and art — the final line-up is confirmed closer to the festival.

Who is eligible to participate?

Participation is open to enrolled students of the college. Individual events may have additional rules (for example, year restrictions or qualification rounds) — these are listed on each event card and confirmed by the committee.

Pending confirmation Exact eligibility criteria per event are still being finalised.

Do I need a team, or can I enter solo?

Both are supported. Each event card shows whether it is individual, team, or either. For team events you will enter a team name and list your teammates during registration.

Is there a registration fee? Pending confirmation

Registration through this website is free. Any entry fee for specific events, along with deadlines, will be confirmed by the organising committee and published here before registration closes.

Will I receive certificates or prizes? Pending confirmation

We will not promise anything that has not been approved. Certificates, prizes and judging criteria will be announced officially once the committee confirms them.

How do I contact the organisers?

Use the details in the Contact section. Include your name, department and the event you are asking about so we can help quickly.
